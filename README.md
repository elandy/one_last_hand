&quot;One Last Hand&quot; is an active item mod for The Binding of Isaac: Repentance that scans the room for eligible pickups (Tarot cards, Soul stones, Runes, and Pills) and evaluates them using poker hand logic. 

Activate the item to play your best hand, trigger powerful permanent stat upgrades, or spawn high-tier item choices directly on the floor!

This item belongs to the Treasure Room and Shop pools.

### Features:
* **Poker Hand Evaluation:** Dynamically detects hands from a Pair up to a Royal Flush based on the cards, runes, and pills currently sitting on the floor.
* **HUD Overlay:** Displays your current best hand and its reward live on screen so you always know what you're playing for.
* **Wildcards:** Supports jokers and wild cards to help fill out your winning hands!
* **Diverse Rewards:** 
  * **Pairs &amp; Triples:** Grant permanent stat ups (Tears, Damage, Luck).
  * **Flushes &amp; Full Houses:** Spawn Breakfast pedestals or duplicate remaining pickups.
  * **Four &amp; Five of a Kind / Royal Flush:** Spawn high-value Treasure Room, Secret Room, and Angel/Devil item choice pools utilizing native Isaac options grouping (pick one, remove the rest).</description>
