&quot;One Last Hand&quot; is an active item mod for The Binding of Isaac: Repentance that scans the room for eligible pickups (Tarot cards, Soul stones, Runes, and Pills) and evaluates them using poker hand logic. 

Activate the item to play your best hand, trigger powerful permanent stat upgrades, or spawn high-tier item choices directly on the floor!

This item belongs to the Treasure Room and Shop pools.

### Features:
* **Poker Hand Evaluation:** Dynamically detects hands from a Pair up to a Royal Flush based on the cards, runes, and pills currently sitting on the floor.
* **HUD Overlay:** Displays your current best hand and its reward live on screen so you always know what you're playing for.
* **Wildcards:** Supports jokers and wild cards to help fill out your winning hands!
* **Diverse Rewards:** 
  * **Pairs &amp; Triples:** Grant permanent stat ups (Tears, Damage, Luck).
  * **Flushes &amp; Full Houses:** Spawn Breakfast pedestals or duplicate remaining pickups.
  * **Four &amp; Five of a Kind / Royal Flush:** Spawn high-value Treasure Room, Secret Room, and Angel/Devil item choice pools utilizing native Isaac options grouping (pick one, remove the rest).</description>

### Item & Trinket Synergies

* **Tarot Cloth (Passive Item)**
  * **Stats:** Doubles permanent stat gains.
  * **Flush:** Upgrades the reward from Breakfast to a guaranteed Treasure Room item.
  * **Full House:** Spawns **2x duplicate copies** of every other eligible pickup.
  * **Choices:** Adds **+1 extra pedestal option** to every choice reward group (4 Treasure options, 3 Secret options, or 3-way Angel/Devil pools).

* **Booster Pack (Passive Item)**
  * Playing any large 4-card or 5-card hand refunds **1 random consumed pickup** back onto the floor.

* **Echo Chamber (Passive Item)**
  * Remembers your single previously played hand and **re-triggers its reward** alongside your current hand upon activation.

* **Moving Box (Active Item)**
  * Increases room storage capacity by 50% while holding *One Last Hand*—allowing you to pack and unpack up to **15 pickups** instead of the vanilla 10 limit.

* **Myosotis (Trinket)**
  * Features a **50% chance** upon entering a new floor to duplicate 1–2 of the eligible cards or pills carried over into the starting room.

* **D1 (Active Item)**
  * Activating *One Last Hand* while holding D1 has a **50% chance** to trigger a **double activation** (granting the hand's rewards twice, with extra choice pedestals split into independent groups). 
  * Using D1 has a 50% chance to spawn an extra exact duplicate of one of the room's pickups.

* **Deck of Cards (Active Item)**
  * **Two-Way Synergy:** Activating *One Last Hand* while holding Deck of Cards has a **50% chance** to trigger a double activation (spawning double rewards/choice groups); activating *Deck of Cards* while holding *One Last Hand* has a corresponding 50% chance to double its active effect as well.
